# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients (YouTube channel owners, brand managers, content studios) deciding whether to hire a video editor. They arrive from referrals or social. They spend 60–90 seconds judging quality and professionalism before reaching out.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio site for Maxim Focenoff, video editor. Works with channels at 2M+ subscribers. Primary conversion: client sends a Telegram message. The site must communicate craft quality and professional reliability faster than a competitor's reel.

## Brand Personality

Serious, professional, laconic. Lets the work speak. No hype, no decoration that isn't earned. The brand feels like the editor's cut: precise, intentional, nothing wasted.

## Anti-references

- Template portfolio-agency sites: Framer/Webflow scaffolds with 01/02/03 section markers, neutral beige palette, tracked uppercase eyebrows above every heading.
- Gamer-RGB aesthetic: neon accents, dark backgrounds with colored glow, streamer energy.
- Corporate LinkedIn style: business photo, navy/blue palette, "professionalism" headlines.
- Warm editorial minimalism: beige/linen bg, display serif italic, Notion/Bear aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. Work is the hero. Every design decision either showcases the reel or gets out of the way.
2. One ask. The page has one conversion goal: start a Telegram conversation. Reduce everything else.
3. Earned restraint. Whitespace and simplicity are not decoration; they signal confidence. Don't fill space to fill it.
4. Serious without cold. Professional tone, not corporate distance. The personality is in the craft, not in friendly copy.
5. Load fast, play immediately. The audience is time-poor; video should start on interaction, not after three clicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion already implemented. All interactive elements have ARIA labels. Color contrast must pass 4.5:1 for all body text.
